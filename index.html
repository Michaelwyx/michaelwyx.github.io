Yixuan Wang | 王怿轩

I am a second-year Master of Science in Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), advised by Prof. Wenzhen Yuan. My research interests include tactile sensing and robotic manipulation, with a focus on contact-rich and dexterous manipulation.

Previously, I received a dual bachelor's degree (B.S. in Mathematics and Physics and B.E. in Mechanical Engineering) from Tsinghua University, where I worked with Huazhe Xu on visuomotor policy learning. I was also fortunate to be mentored by Yao Jiang, who introduced me to research.
